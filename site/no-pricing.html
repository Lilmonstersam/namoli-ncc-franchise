Select Your Specialist Path

Start with office cleaning, then add sectors as you grow. Namoli franchisees are trained to operate in high-stakes environments, allowing you to secure higher-value contracts than general cleaners.

Office Cleaning

The backbone of commercial cleaning: recurring, after-hours contracts for corporate offices. Security-cleared teams deliver consistent, non-disruptive cleaning built around client schedules and high-traffic areas.

Medical & Healthcare

Strict adherence to infection control protocols. We protect patients and staff with hospital-grade sanitisation and documented compliance for medical centres.

Education & Childcare

Safe, hygienic learning environments using non-toxic products. Our staff are fully vetted with Working With Children Checks to ensure student safety.

Industrial & Retail

Robust OHS-focused cleaning for warehouses and retail. We manage hazards and high-traffic grime with specialised machinery and safety-first procedures.
